# Jade scroll artwork

`scroll-rollers-jade-v1.png` is an original transparent RGBA atlas generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The original image is preserved without file cropping or resampling. `src/renderer/pet/seatedScrollArt.ts` selects the open and rolled states with Pixi texture frames and stretches only the central shaft, preserving the carved ends.

- Open texture frame: x=0, y=350, width=1254, height=164; shaft center y=83.
- Rolled texture frame: x=0, y=735, width=1254, height=170; shaft center y=84.
- End sections: 185 source pixels each. Display scale follows the scroll width at a 22:1 length/height ratio, clamped to 0.1–0.35.
- Both upper and lower axes remain visible in both states. Replies retain their separate speech bubble.
